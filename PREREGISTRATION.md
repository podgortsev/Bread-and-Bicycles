## Registered expectations

**Confirms the thesis.** The FORM x FORM interaction is negative
(super-additive) and clears the wrapper spread, AND the STATED x STATED
interaction is not distinguishable from zero, in at least two of three models
and on at least two of three channels.

**Refutes the thesis.** The STATED x STATED interaction is super-additive, or
the FORM x FORM interaction is absent, in two of three models on the channels
that are powered for it.

**A null that is still the paper.** No interaction of either type is detectable
anywhere, and the two-neutral-clause floor shows that clause count alone
explains what movement there is. Then the paper reports that stacking does not
happen at this scale on these instruments, with the minimum detectable effect
stated, and the field's compounding claim is challenged on the evidence rather
than replaced.

**What would make the whole thing uninterpretable**, and must therefore be
checked before anything is claimed: the CONTROL_ALT x2 cell differing from
CONTROL_ALT by as much as a real signal does, which would mean the instrument
measures clause count. That check runs first, in every channel.

---

## Which of the three outcomes occurred

The third. No compounding of either type is detectable, and the floor shows that
the movement other instruments report is not about people. The working thesis,
that form signals compound while stated signals do not, is retired in the paper
rather than defended; the paper says so in the discussion and in the abstract.

The uninterpretability check did not merely pass as a formality. It failed on
four of the five instruments, which is how the paper's subject changed from the
stacking question to the measurement question.

## Deviations from this registration, declared

- **The stratified floor check was added after seeing the first A2 analysis.**
  It can only remove claims, and it was validated on synthetic data built to
  reproduce the failure before being applied, but it was not registered in
  advance. Stated here and in the paper's limitations.
- **The task set grew from 200 items to 600** after the offline validator
  measured 20 percent power for an interaction at the smaller size. The change
  was made before collection and for a reason that has nothing to do with any
  observed result.
